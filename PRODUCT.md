# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
[INFERRED] Solo founders, freelancers, or small sales teams who need a fast, low-friction way to manage leads and deals daily without the bloat of traditional enterprise CRMs.

## Product Purpose
[INFERRED] A sleek, high-craft operating system ("LEAD OS") for capturing, sorting, and converting leads into closed deals efficiently. Success means users spend less time managing the tool and more time closing deals.

## Positioning
[INFERRED] Consumer-grade aesthetics and speed applied to a B2B sales workflow. LEAD OS refuses the typical enterprise data-entry grind in favor of a delightful, focused operating experience.

## Operating Context
[INFERRED] Fast-paced daily check-ins, rapid data entry during or immediately after client calls, and quick pipeline reviews.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Confirmed functionality: Leads data grid with sorting, Dashboard with revenue and source metrics.
Technical constraints: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, Recharts for visualizations.

## Brand Commitments
Name: "LEAD OS".
